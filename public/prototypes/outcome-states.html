Behavior · Table Assignment

The happy path, and where it breaks

Two paths. One is automatic: the system places the party and it’s done. The other begins when it can’t, and a person takes over, reading the floor and overriding when they know more than the system does.

Automatic
The system assigns a fitting table with no human input. This is the outcome most reservations resolve to.
When the system can’t, staff take over
Auto-assign returns nothing, so the floor opens up and a person makes the call, overriding an upcoming or occupied table when they know it’s actually flexible.
